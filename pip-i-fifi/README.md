# Pip & Fifi: English → Polish game for Olivia (iPad)

Open in Safari: https://sw-dev-kamil.github.io/polski/pip-i-fifi/ → Share → Add to Home Screen.

- **Games:** Colours, Animals, Counting, Playing with friends, At nursery, Sticker book.
- **Parent settings:** press and hold the ⚙️ (top right of the menu) for 1.5 s. You can change the voice style (Natural / Cartoon / Extra), turn the microphone on or off, and edit her name.
- **Content:** all words and phrases are in `content.js`. After editing, bump `pipfifi-v1` in `sw.js`.
- **Tip:** turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access) so she can't leave the app; triple-click the top button to start it.
